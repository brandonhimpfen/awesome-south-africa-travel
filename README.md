## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, visa guidelines, and weather tracking.

- [South African Tourism (Official Portal)](https://www.southafrica.net/) - Official national tourism board guide featuring destination highlights, travel itineraries, and cultural guides.
- [Department of Home Affairs - Visas](https://www.dha.gov.za/) - Official government portal for checking tourist visa entry requirements, passport regulations, and immigration rules.
- [South African Weather Service (SAWS)](https://www.weathersa.co.za/) - Official national meteorological agency providing regional weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national rail networks, luxury tourist trains, domestic aviation, and car rental infrastructure.

- [South African Airways (SAA)](https://www.flysaa.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic routes and regional African connections.
- [Airlink](https://www.flyairlink.com/) - Major regional airline providing extensive connectivity between major metropolitan hubs and safari airstrips.
- [Rovos Rail / Blue Train](https://www.rovos.com/) - World-renowned luxury tourist train operators offering scenic rail journeys across South Africa's historic routes.

## Destinations & Regions

### Cape Town & Western Cape

- [Cape Town Tourism (Official City Portal)](https://www.capetown.travel/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring Table Mountain, V&A Waterfront, Cape Peninsula, and wine estates.
- [SANParks Table Mountain National Park](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain) - Official administration portal for hiking trails, Cape Point, and protected floral reserves.
- [Wines of South Africa (WOSA)](https://www.wosa.co.za/) - Official visitor guide for exploring historic wine regions in Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, and Paarl.

### Johannesburg & Gauteng

- [Gauteng Tourism Authority](https://www.gauteng.net/) - Official regional visitor guide for exploring Johannesburg, Soweto heritage sites, and the Cradle of Humankind.
- [Constitution Hill Human Rights Precinct](https://www.constitutionhill.org.za/) - Official visitor portal for the historic fort and prison complex overlooking Johannesburg.

### Kruger National Park & Lowveld

- [SANParks Kruger National Park](https://www.sanparks.org/parks/kruger) - Official official booking and visitor portal for South Africa's premier wildlife reserve, rest camps, and game viewing routes.

### Garden Route & Eastern Cape

- [Garden Route Tourism Portal](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/) - Official regional guide for coastal towns, Knysna lagoon, Tsitsikamma National Park, and coastal forests.

### KwaZulu-Natal & Drakensberg

- [Tourism KwaZulu-Natal](https://www.zulu.org.za/) - Official regional portal for exploring Durban's beachfront, the Battlefields route, and uKhahlamba-Drakensberg Park.
- [Ezemvelo KZN Wildlife](https://www.kznwildlife.com/) - Official provincial agency managing game reserves, Hluhluwe–iMfolozi Park, and Drakensberg mountain hiking routes.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, medical assistance, and traveler safety protocols.

- [South African Police Service (SAPS) Emergency (Dial 10111)](https://www.sps.gov.za/) - Direct national emergency dispatch number for police assistance.
- [National Ambulance & Fire Emergency (Dial 10177)](https://www.gov.za/) - Direct emergency dispatch for medical rescue and fire services.
